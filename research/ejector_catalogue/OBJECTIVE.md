# Full objective and stopping claims

The subsequent [catalogue decision campaign](../ejector_search/README.md)
certifies continuous-ODE exclusions and existence witnesses for every triple
in explicit finite, bounded measurement/model domains. This closes the
Monte-Carlo/optimizer exclusion gap for those domains. It does not satisfy all
six requirements below or enumerate all possible origins of a positive group.

The original objective is a precomputed traceback for every fast star in the
all-survey fused astrometric catalogue, and every common space-time origin of
several such stars, with what is visible there.

Full completion requires all of the following:

1. A documented, available all-survey input catalogue and fast-star selection;
   coverage and exclusion accounting, including missing radial velocities.
2. Tracebacks for every eligible input source, with astrometric, distance,
   radial-velocity, solar-frame and Galactic-potential uncertainty.
3. A search that does not silently miss common origins through catalogue,
   group, posterior-sample or time discretization truncation.
4. Selection-aware false-association calibration and recovery checks on both
   real known systems and injections into the real background.
5. Visibility/source matching at the inferred historical origin, with survey
   coverage and completeness, rather than assuming an unmatched source is dark.
6. Reproducible evidence establishing which reported candidates are ejections;
   independent confirmation before claiming a new astronomical discovery.

This campaign attempts a real all-sky Gaia selection, every usable source in
that selection, continuous interpolation encounters, all fixed-size groups,
uncertainty sensitivity, precomputed trajectories, and real-background
injection recovery. Those are concrete advances toward the objective.
They do not make all six requirements true. The full-objective flag must remain
false unless evidence satisfies them, irrespective of implementation quality or
how interesting a candidate appears. Negative results are legitimate outcomes.
